## Cortical geometry and atlas

`public/brain/fsaverage5.json` was generated by `scripts/prepare-brain.py` using Nilearn 0.14.1:

- fsaverage5 pial coordinates, triangles, and sulcal depth from Nilearn’s packaged FreeSurfer data: https://github.com/nilearn/nilearn/tree/main/nilearn/datasets/data/fsaverage5
- Destrieux surface annotations retrieved by `fetch_atlas_surf_destrieux` from NITRC: https://www.nitrc.org/frs/download.php/9343/lh.aparc.a2009s.annot and https://www.nitrc.org/frs/download.php/9342/rh.aparc.a2009s.annot
- Destrieux C, Fischl B, Dale A, Halgren E. Automatic parcellation of human cortical gyri and sulci using standard anatomical nomenclature. NeuroImage 53(1), 2010, 1–15. DOI: 10.1016/j.neuroimage.2010.06.010.
- Fischl B, Sereno MI, Dale AM. Cortical surface-based analysis II: Inflation, flattening, and a surface-based coordinate system. NeuroImage 9(2), 1999, 195–207.

The Nilearn dataset description reports the fsaverage5 data license as unknown; attribution is not a substitute for checking data redistribution terms for downstream use. Geometry is rounded to three decimals. Vertex ordering and triangle topology are preserved. Atlas indices are copied directly, not inferred from geometric location.
